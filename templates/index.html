Real Estate GST Statutory Rules & CA Directives

Authoritative real estate statutory rules codified into the automation engine for Sun Builders Projects LLP

calendar_today 1. Pre-BU vs Post-BU Exemption Cutoff

Legal Authority: Schedule III, Entry 5 of the CGST Act, 2017 (Sale of Land and Completed Building).

CA Directive: For Sun Footprint, BU (Building Use / Completion) permission was granted as on 18/03/2025.

Advances/collections received AFTER 18/03/2025 = 100% EXEMPT from GST.
Advances/collections received BEFORE 18/03/2025 = TAXABLE (1% / 5%).

filter_alt 2. 5 Mandatory Non-GST Deductions

Legal Authority: Rule 33 of CGST Rules (Pure Agent) & Section 15 Valuation Rules.

GST is strictly NOT calculated on:

  • Refundable Security Deposit (Not consideration for construction)
  • Stamp Duty (Cr/Dr) (Pure agent statutory fee reimbursement)
  • Sub-Registrar Registration (Cr/Dr) (Pure agent payment)
  • Maintenance Deposit / Expenses (Held in trust for society)
  • Electricity Expenses (AEC / AUDA / Torrent actual cost reimbursement)
percent 3. Residential Rate Slabs (Affordable vs Others)

Legal Authority: Notification No. 03/2019-Central Tax (Rate).

  • Affordable (≤ ₹45 Lakhs): 1% GST without ITC (Sun Atmosphere).
  • Non-Affordable (> ₹45 Lakhs): 5% GST without ITC (Sun Park West, Sun Silver Spring).
  • Commercial Shops in RREP: 5% GST without ITC.
store 4. Commercial Other & 1/3rd Land Abatement

Legal Authority: Notification No. 11/2017-Central Tax (Rate) as amended.

For standalone commercial projects like Sun Gravitas Commercial, gross consideration is taxed at 18% with a mandatory 1/3rd deduction for deemed value of land:

Taxable Value = Total Gross Consideration × (2/3)
Formula: =B8/3 (Land Deduction) | Effective GST Rate = 12.00%
